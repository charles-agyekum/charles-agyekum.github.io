Strategic Default Pressure Index — advance-funding risk

When you lend against a future delivery, at what market price does your counterparty profit by not delivering? For this book: a 6.00% premium — derived from the contract's own deduction structure, not assumed. Rebuilt from the operational records of the cocoa franchise I ran under personal bond: 36 purchasing clerks, 16 weeks, GHS 1.66M funded. Peak open exposure of 654 bags closed 83% by season end, and 4 of 36 counterparties carried half the gap — which is why the funding policy was tiered, not uniform.

forfeit = buffer + commission
  # 36 + 12 GHS per 800 bag
threshold = forfeit / price
  # 6.00% — derived, exact
# above it, default pays

DataCo Late Delivery — rebuilt in pandas

The same supply-chain finding as the flagship dashboard, taken back to raw data in Python. I derived “late” from the shipping dates myself instead of trusting the vendor’s label — and the raw rule flagged 4,423 more orders, all cancelled shipments already behind schedule. Same headline, verified from first principles: 54.8% late, $2.14M of profit exposed.

View the notebook →
late = df["days_real"] > df["days_sched"]
late.sum()  # 103,400
late.mean() # 0.548
df.loc[late,"profit"].sum()
   # $2.14M at risk
